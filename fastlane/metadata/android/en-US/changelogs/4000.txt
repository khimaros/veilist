* the checkbox toggles an item between open and complete; press and hold an
  item to pick another state (active or blocked)
* join a list by scanning its qr code with the camera
* a shared list you have just joined no longer says "synced" while it is still
  arriving, and no longer shows a half-built list that then rewrites itself
* fixed a list appearing to bounce between states: a slow copy from the network
  could overwrite newer data
* edits now order by a logical clock, so a change made after seeing someone
  else's change always wins - even if the two devices' clocks disagree
* lighter app icon
* releases are signed with a persistent key from here on, so future versions
  update in place
