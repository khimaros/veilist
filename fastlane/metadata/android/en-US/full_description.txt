make a list, share it with a link, and everyone with the link edits it together.

veilist has no server. every list lives as a record on the veilid network, and
the devices holding the link read and write it directly, so no operator ever
sees a list, its contents, or who is editing it. there is no account to create
and nothing to sign in to.

what it does:

* keep track of every list you have made or been given
* add, rename, reorder and delete items; tap the checkbox to tick an item off,
  or press and hold it to mark it active or blocked
* share a list as a link or a qr code, and join one by scanning that code
* collaborate live: edits from everyone with the link merge as they arrive, and
  each person's changes stay attributable to them
* work offline first: lists are created and edited on the device straight away
  and sync when the network comes back
* hide completed items when a list gets long

a list you create stays on your device until the first time you share it, so an
unshared list never touches the network at all.

the same app also runs in a browser at veilid.tech/list, where it starts a
veilid node in the page via webassembly - so someone without the app installed
can still open a link you send them without a gateway server in between.

veilist is free software (GPL-3.0). the source, the build recipe and the pinned
toolchain are at https://github.com/khimaros/veilist
